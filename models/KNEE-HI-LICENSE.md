## Changes

All original vertices and triangles retained. Common registered transform [x/1000,z/1000+0.0781112,y/1000-0.00222675]; winding reversed; unit normals quantized to int16. No per-object offsets, mirroring, smoothing, decimation or segmentation.
Positions are little-endian float32 metres, normals int16 scaled by 32767, indices uint32, buffers aligned to four bytes.
The eight source objects are separate bilateral source files. No side is generated by mirroring.

## Scope and limits

Original-resolution here means every triangle of the selected pinned OBJ is retained, not clinical high detail. Patella sources have only 178/180 vertices and are barely finer than the old 176/177-vertex atlas objects. No cartilage, meniscus, ligament, articular segmentation or dynamic joint simulation is supplied. Anatomical congruence is not certified.
